01 /CHEMISTRY INVESTIGATIONAPR 2026

Galvanic Cell
Voltage Investigation

Exploring how copper sulfate concentration changes the voltage of a zinc–copper cell, one controlled experiment at a time.

25experimental trials
5concentrations
ElectrochemistryExcelData analysis
Explore the investigationClose the investigationPROBLEM → PROCESS → RESULTS

01 / Problem

How does copper(II) ion concentration affect the voltage produced by a zinc–copper galvanic cell?

02 / Approach

Investigated five copper sulfate concentrations from 0.10 to 0.50 mol/L across 25 trials. Compared the mean voltages and examined voltage against logarithmic concentration.

03 / Experimental setup

Two half-cells were connected by a salt bridge, with electrodes connected to a digital multimeter. Volumetric glassware was used to prepare the solutions.

04 / My contribution

Prepared solutions, conducted voltage measurements, and processed the results in Microsoft Excel. Used graphs, trendlines, and uncertainty bars to present the findings.

05 / RESULTS

A small voltage change.
A measurable pattern.

Mean voltage increased overall from approximately 1.01 V to 1.04 V across the tested concentration range. The report’s voltage-versus-log-concentration fit gave R² = 0.7947.

Overlapping uncertainty bars limited how clearly some concentration levels could be distinguished.

06 / Skills & tools

Solution preparation · Volumetric glassware · Digital multimeter · Microsoft Excel · Regression analysis · Measurement uncertainty

02 /PHYSICS INVESTIGATIONAPR 2026

01 / Problem

How does the release angle of a pendulum-mounted golf club affect the calculated coefficient of restitution during a collision with a golf ball?

02 / Approach

Conducted 40 trials across eight release angles from 10° to 80°, keeping the club, ball, and contact surface constant. Repeated the collision at each angle and compared the processed measurements.

03 / Experimental setup

A fixed pivot supported the golf club. A protractor set the release angle, a photogate measured the ball’s motion after impact, and a camera recorded the club’s motion.

04 / My contribution

Carried out the collision trials, collected photogate and video measurements, and used the data to investigate the coefficient of restitution and the limitations of the experimental model.

05 / RESULTS

The limitations were
part of the learning.

The calculated coefficient of restitution ranged from approximately 0.19 to 0.55. The experimental linearised fit was weak (R² ≈ 0.23), so the results did not establish close agreement with the model.

The report identified alignment and repeatability as possible limitations to investigate.

06 / Skills & tools

Photogate sensors · Video measurements · Experimental design · Mathematical modelling · Uncertainty evaluation · Technical reporting
